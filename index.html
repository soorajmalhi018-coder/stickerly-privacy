Stickerly – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Stickerly is a photo sticker maker for Android. It has no account system, no backend server, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores locally

  • Photos you import, and the stickers you create, saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your favorites and sticker history, saved in on-device storage.

What the app never does

  • It never uploads your photos or stickers anywhere.
  • It never creates an account or collects personal information.
  • It never shares data with third parties.
  • It shows no ads and contains no trackers.

Permissions

  • Photos / media: used only to let you pick an image to turn into a sticker.
  • Background removal runs entirely on-device using Google ML Kit's on-device models. No image data leaves your phone.

WhatsApp

Sending stickers to WhatsApp uses WhatsApp's own sticker-pack integration on your device. Stickerly is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, WhatsApp or Meta.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Either permanently removes everything Stickerly has stored on your device. Because nothing is stored on any server, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Stickerly does not collect data from anyone, including children.
